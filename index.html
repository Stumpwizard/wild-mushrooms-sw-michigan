What the edibility labels mean

✓ Edible speciesEaten when correctly identified and appropriately prepared. This does not mean a photographed specimen is safe to eat.

✕ ToxicPoisonous; do not eat.

✕ Nonedible — too toughTurkey tail is unsuitable for eating whole, without being classified as poisonous.

Scope: This is a curated regional guide, not a census of every species in southwest Michigan. Many groups require microscopy or DNA for species level identification. The seasonal windows reflect typical southwest Michigan fruiting, not a current forecast. Months are approximate and shift with rain and temperature. Common names vary by region and may refer to more than one species. Photos show examples, never a complete identification.

Safety: Toxic and deadly species are included for recognition. Never use an app, one photo, or an edibility label to decide what to eat. Michigan DNR recommends learning with an experienced guide. DNR identification guidance · Foraging rules · Berrien County observations.

Example images and attribution are supplied by iNaturalist taxon photos when available. A representative species photo may show a specimen from outside Michigan.

Before you use this guide

A wrong mushroom identification can be fatal.

  • For learning and comparison only. Photos, descriptions, feature matches and green “Edible species” checks cannot confirm that your mushroom is safe to eat.
  • Deadly lookalikes can appear nearly identical. Mistakes can cause severe poisoning, organ failure or death. This guide is incomplete; an absent warning does not mean a mushroom is safe.
  • Cooking does not make poisonous mushrooms safe. Symptoms of poisoning can be delayed for hours.
  • Never decide to eat a mushroom using this guide alone. Have every specimen identified in person by a qualified mushroom expert before considering consumption.

Suspected mushroom poisoning? In the U.S., call Poison Control immediately at 1-800-222-1222. Do not wait for symptoms.

Call 911 if someone collapses, has a seizure, has trouble breathing or cannot be awakened.
